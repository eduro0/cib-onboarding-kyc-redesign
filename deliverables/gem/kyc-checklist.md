# KYC checklist (illustrative)

Illustrative policy built from public Hong Kong rules for a portfolio demo. Not any real bank's policy.

## Beneficial owners
A beneficial owner is an individual who owns or controls, directly or indirectly, more than 25% of the company's
shares or voting rights (AMLO, Cap. 615, Schedule 2) [src:hk-amlo-sch2] [src:hkma-aml-cft-guideline-2023]. A person who
exercises ultimate control over the management is also a beneficial owner, whatever they own. Multiply percentages
along each chain of companies.

## Documents for the company (onboarding)
- Certificate of incorporation
- Business registration certificate (current)
- Articles of association
- Significant controllers register, which every Hong Kong company must keep (Companies Ordinance, Cap. 622) and banks
  cannot search, so the client provides it [src:cr-significant-controllers-register]
- Register of directors, or a registry extract or annual return listing them
- Register of members, or a registry extract or annual return listing them

## Documents for each company in the ownership chain
- Certificate of incorporation or a registry extract, so the bank knows it exists as stated
- Register of members, so the chain can be followed to the individuals

## Documents for each director and each beneficial owner
- Identity document (passport or ID card), not expired
- Proof of address dated within the last 3 months (policy choice for this demo)

## Periodic review
High-risk clients are reviewed at least once a year, and a change in beneficial ownership or control is a trigger
for an earlier review (HKMA AML/CFT Guideline, paras 4.12.2 and 5.3) [src:hkma-aml-cft-guideline-2023].
- Compare the registry and new documents with the bank's file: directors, shareholdings, registered address, name.
- Ask the client only for what changed or expired: ID and address for new directors and new beneficial owners,
  an updated significant controllers register when ownership changed, and ownership evidence for any new company
  in the chain.
- If nothing changed and nothing expired, the analyst may confirm a no-change review without contacting the client.
  The HKMA expects requests not to duplicate information the bank already holds or that is public, and identities
  already verified need not be re-verified without a reason to doubt them [src:hkma-rba-cdd-2024]
  [src:hkma-aml-cft-guideline-2023].
